      What is disposable temporary email?

      A disposable temporary email is a free email address that you use for a short time and then throw away. You do not need to give your real email address to every website that asks for one. Sign up with a temporary address, receive the verification code here, and walk away — no spam in your real inbox, ever.

      Temporary email is receive-only: you can read messages that arrive, but you cannot send mail from a temporary address. That is exactly what makes it safe for one-time sign-ups.

      Frequently asked questions

      Is Temp Mail really free?

      Yes. Creating an address, receiving mail and switching addresses are all free, with no account and no limits on how many addresses you make.

      Do I need to sign up or give my real email?

      No. There is no sign-up at all. Your address is created instantly when you open the page.

      How long do messages stay in the inbox?

      Messages are kept for a short time only — usually a few hours — and then deleted automatically. Copy any code you need right away.

      Can I send email from a temporary address?

      No. Temporary addresses are receive-only. You can read incoming mail, but you cannot send replies or new messages.

      Can I choose my own address name?

      Yes. Type a name in the custom-name box, pick a domain, and press "Create custom address". Dots are removed from names automatically.

      Why did a website reject my temporary address?

      Some websites block well-known temporary-mail domains. If that happens, try creating a new address with a different domain from the domain picker.

      Is it safe to use for important accounts?

      No. Never use a temporary address for banking, money, password recovery or anything you cannot afford to lose. Use your real email for important accounts.

      Do you store my emails?

      Messages live only as long as the address exists and are deleted automatically after a short time. Your previous addresses are saved only in your own browser (localStorage) so you can switch back to them.

      Advertising

      How do real ads arrive on a site like this? In plain words: first the site needs its own domain with HTTPS, then the owner opens a free account with an ad network — for example Monetag or Adsterra, which approve new sites quickly, or Google AdSense, which has a stricter review — and finally the network gives a small code snippet that is now live in the two ad slots on this page. Visitors now see real ads, and the money from those ads collects in the ad-network account. Monetag and Adsterra pay out from as little as $5; AdSense pays out from $100.
